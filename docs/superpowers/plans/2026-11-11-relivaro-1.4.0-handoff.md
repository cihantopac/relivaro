Facts specific to this release:
- **A detection fixture fails on any finding that has no expectation.** Every false-positive fix therefore needs a negative case in a fixture (`tests/integration/detection.test.ts`, harness `scripts/lib/fixtures.mjs`).
- **The generic test downgrade** is at `src/rules/engine.ts:201-204`. Non-`testing` rules without the `test-aware` tag drop one severity step in test files and go to low confidence. Task 4 excludes two rules completely instead.
- **`isSafePart`** (`src/rules/helpers.ts:112`) already treats ALL_CAPS and camelCase module constants as safe. The real gaps are the ternary, `.length + 1`, and `.map(...).join(...)`.
  - The doc comment of `boundToLiteral` claims ternaries are handled, but they are not.
- **The `sec/sql-injection` rule** is at `src/rules/security/code.ts:52-125`. An untainted dynamic part is reported as medium severity, low confidence.
- **`sec/docker-context-leak`** (`files.ts:626-672`) returns early when the audit root has a `.dockerignore`. That silences every Dockerfile, which is a false negative that Task 2 removes.
- **`FileSet` cannot read above the audit root** (`readAny` resolves against `realRoot`). That is why Task 1 adds a separate, allowlisted ancestor reader.
- **178 vs 177:** `metrics.dependencies` (`audit.ts:392`) sums the raw lockfile entries, while `queryOsv` (`osv.ts:141-151`) deduplicates by ecosystem, name and version.
- **Walk mode** (`--no-git`, or no git repo) reads only the root `.gitignore` (`files.ts:709`). Its skips never reach `SkipStats`.
- **Sensitive files that are not tracked** are removed by the `FileSet` constructor (`files.ts:453`) before any rule sees them. `--assume-tracked` must keep them unread: they are reported by path only.
- **Inline suppressions** are parsed by `SUPPRESS_RE` / `parseSuppressions` at `src/rules/engine.ts:90-121` and applied unconditionally at :207-215.
- **Frameva also asked for features that this plan does not build.** They are covered by planner rulings in the 1.4.0 and 1.6.0 plans; do not add them.
